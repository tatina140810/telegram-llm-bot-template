# Acme Trading LLC — Employee FAQ

All names, numbers and rules in this file are fictional sample data.

**Who approves my expenses?** Your direct manager. If your manager is away for more than 3 days, the finance team approves instead.

**How do I get a company card?** Employees who travel more than twice a quarter can request one from the finance team. Card spending follows the same expense policy and still needs receipts.

**I lost a receipt. What now?** Fill in the missing-receipt form and ask your manager to sign it. This is allowed twice per year.

**Who do I contact with questions?** Write to the finance team at finance@acme.example.
