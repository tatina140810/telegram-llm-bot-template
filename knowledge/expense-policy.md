# Acme Trading LLC — Expense Policy

All names, numbers and rules in this file are fictional sample data.

## Submitting expenses

Submit every business expense within 14 calendar days of the purchase date. Attach a photo or PDF of the receipt. Expenses without a receipt are accepted only up to $25 and only with a written explanation.

Expenses are reviewed by your manager. Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next payroll; rejected expenses come back with a comment and can be corrected and resubmitted.

## Limits

- Meals while travelling: up to $60 per day.
- Client entertainment: up to $150 per guest; requires the client's company name in the description.
- Office supplies: up to $200 per month without pre-approval.
- Software subscriptions must be approved by IT before purchase.

## Not reimbursable

Personal entertainment, alcohol without a client present, traffic fines, upgrades to business class, and gifts above $50.
