# Acme Trading LLC — Travel Policy

All names, numbers and rules in this file are fictional sample data.

## Booking

Book flights at least 14 days in advance through the company travel portal. Economy class is the default; premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours.

Hotels: up to $180 per night in major cities and $130 elsewhere. Stays longer than 5 nights need approval from the department head.

## Ground transport

Use public transport or ride-hailing. A rental car is allowed when it is cheaper than taxis for the whole trip or when visiting several clients in one day. Mileage for a personal car is reimbursed at $0.60 per mile.

## Per diem

Instead of itemised meals you may claim a per diem of $55 per day for domestic trips and $75 for international trips. You cannot combine per diem with meal receipts for the same day.
